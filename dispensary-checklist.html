Dispensary Checklist: 7 Things to Check Before You Go

Quick answer: before you head out, confirm the shop is state-licensed, open right now, and takes your payment type, and bring a valid ID showing you are 21 or older.

  1. License. Make sure the shop is on the state's license list.
  2. Hours. Check today's posted hours. Holidays change them.
  3. ID. Bring an unexpired government ID. Medical patients also bring their card.
  4. Payment. Many shops take cash or debit only. Some have an ATM inside.
  5. Deals. Look for first-time and daily specials before you order.
  6. Pre-order. Ordering ahead skips the line at busy shops.
  7. Getting home. Keep products sealed in the trunk. Never drive impaired.
