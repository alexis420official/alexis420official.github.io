Dispensary Near Me Open Now: How to Find One That's Actually Open

In short: use a finder that checks each store's posted hours against the current time where you are, then look at the closing time, not just the word "open". Most licensed dispensaries open around 9 AM and close between 9 and 10 PM.

Search "dispensary near me open now" on a map app and you'll get pins. Some are smoke shops, some are CBD stores, and some closed an hour ago. The fix is to search only licensed stores and filter by the hours they post. This page walks through how, with the actual numbers.

Why "open" isn't enough

  • Closing time matters more. Most stores stop ringing people up a few minutes before they lock the door. Under 30 minutes left on the clock, call first.
  • State law caps the night. California stops sales at 10 PM, Florida at 9 PM, Hawaii and Kentucky at 8 PM. In those states, "open now" at 10:30 PM is going to be an empty list.
  • Holidays. Posted hours are the normal week. Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day often mean short hours. Delaware bans sales on Thanksgiving, Easter and Christmas outright.

Before you head out

  1. Bring a valid photo ID showing you're 21+, or your patient card in a medical-only state.
  2. Bring cash or debit. Credit cards rarely work for cannabis.
  3. If you're cutting it close, order online for pickup.
